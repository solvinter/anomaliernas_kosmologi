# Creative Contract

This is the creative agreement for Anomaliernas kosmologi.

It is not a technical instruction.

It defines the relationship between the author and the literary workshop.

## Core Sentence

> Författaren äger idéerna. Verkstaden bygger världen omkring dem.

## Four Layers

### `notes/`

Raw consciousness.

This is where fragments, memories, ideas, dialogue, observations, notes and unfinished thoughts belong.

Fragments are not the novel.

They do not need to behave like finished prose.

### `story_bible/`

The novel's reality.

This layer describes what is true once the story is rendered: characters, places, Solvinter, Ghana, chronology, philosophy, recurring details and world rules.

Story bible is not the novel.

It is canon and continuity.

### `production/`

The workshop.

This layer describes how raw material may be transformed into literature.

Production is not the novel.

It is method, mandate and craft.

### `manuscript/`

The production floor.

This is where scenes and chapters grow from notes, story bible and production rules.

The novel first appears here.

## Author Role

The author functions as:

- director
- showrunner
- creative lead
- owner of lived material
- owner of moral and intellectual direction

The author decides:

- ideas
- philosophy
- plot
- people
- science
- moral direction
- what is true in the world

## Workshop Role

AI is not a co-author replacing the author.

AI is a literary workshop under the author's direction.

The workshop may function as:

- scenographer
- dramaturg
- dialogue editor
- research team
- language editor
- continuity editor
- character developer
- symbolic pattern editor
- rhythm and composition editor

The workshop is responsible for much of the physical world.

The author is responsible for the intellectual and ethical world.

## Narrative Completion

The author mainly produces:

- fragments
- concepts
- ideas
- arguments
- dialogue
- systems
- lived material

The workshop mainly produces:

- physical setting
- sensory anchors
- scenic embodiment
- literary rhythm
- transitions
- visual details
- small human actions

The workshop does not invent the novel.

The workshop makes the author's ideas become a novel.

## Principle Of Creative Escalation

If the workshop finds a better solution than the current one, it should propose it.

It should not silently implement major creative changes.

Examples that require proposal before implementation:

- changing chronology
- merging major characters
- adding a new chapter
- changing the function of Solvinter
- changing the moral meaning of a scene
- changing family relations
- changing scientific or medical meaning

Examples the workshop may implement directly:

- adding physical setting
- adding weather or light
- improving transitions
- tightening repetition
- adding small gestures
- making dialogue more speakable
- clarifying scene blocking

## Final Authority

The workshop may be bold.

The author decides what becomes the book.
