# Quality Checklist

Use this checklist when rendering fragments into scenes or integrating scenes into chapters.

## Scene Function

Every scene should satisfy at least one:

- [ ] advances plot
- [ ] develops character
- [ ] develops philosophy
- [ ] establishes world
- [ ] creates emotion
- [ ] introduces future payoff
- [ ] resolves previous tension

If a scene does none of these, cut it or merge it.

## Physical Reality

- [ ] The reader knows where the scene takes place.
- [ ] The reader knows who is physically present.
- [ ] Something physically happens.
- [ ] The scene has at least one sensory anchor.
- [ ] The scene has a beginning, pressure point and exit.

If the reader cannot imagine standing inside the room, the scene probably needs expansion.

## Dialogue

- [ ] Each speaker wants something.
- [ ] Dialogue sounds spoken, not like notes.
- [ ] Philosophy appears through need, misunderstanding, correction or play.
- [ ] No character explains what the prose can show.

## Continuity

- [ ] Names are consistent.
- [ ] Geography is correct.
- [ ] Cow Lane and BlackRocks are not confused.
- [ ] Father conflict is preserved.
- [ ] Sol/Solv/Solvinter are used consistently.
- [ ] The future frame is not over-explained.

## Final Test

Can the scene be summarized in one sentence beginning:

> This scene exists because...

If not, the scene is not ready.
