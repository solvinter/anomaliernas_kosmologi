# Film Manifesto

Every chapter should be imaginable as a film.

Every important scene should have a physical location.

Readers should always know where they are.

Readers should know who is present.

Readers should know what is physically happening.

Readers should feel why the scene exists.

Philosophy should emerge through people, places and action whenever possible.

The reader should never feel that they are reading an essay disguised as a novel.

## Practical Rule

If a scene cannot be visualized, it probably needs either:

- a place
- a body
- an object
- a sound
- a change in posture
- a visible action
- a clearer reason to exist

## Final Image Test

After reading a scene, the reader should remember at least one image.

If no image remains, the scene may still contain an idea, but it has not yet become fully cinematic prose.
